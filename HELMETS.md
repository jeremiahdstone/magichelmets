# Magic Helmets

Abilities apply while the matching helm is worn.

All four helms provide the same armor protection as a diamond helmet: 3 armor and 2 armor toughness.

## Builder's Helm
- Haste I and Jump Boost II.
- Adds 4.5 blocks of block interaction range.
- Reduces fall damage by 50%.
- Increases underwater mining speed to 5x its base value.

## Keeper's Helm
- Luck I and Hero of the Village I.
- Regeneration I when within 24 blocks of a villager.
- Every 5 minutes, advances crops in a 25 × 5 × 25 area around the wearer by one growth stage.

## Wanderer's Helm
- Night Vision in low light.
- Water Breathing and Speed I.
- Reduces fall damage by 50%.

## Warrior's Helm
- Strength I and +0.4 knockback resistance.
- While sneaking, gives nearby hostile mobs and players within 24 blocks the glowing effect.
- Second Wind: at 4.5 hearts or less, grants Resistance II and Speed I for 3 seconds, with a 30-second cooldown.
